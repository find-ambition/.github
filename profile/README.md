# Find Ambition

**A software studio and agency: we build for our clients, and we build our own.**

Find Ambition designs, builds and runs software. We take on client projects and
grow our own products alongside them, held to the same engineering standards.

## Projects

### Tanikala

**Technology for Filipinos, connected through one shared ecosystem.**

Focused applications that help people connect, learn, share and support one
another. Each has a distinct purpose, and all share one platform identity,
safety standards and engineering foundation.

| Application | Purpose | Product direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Convos** | Connect | Meaningful conversations and new connections |
| **Pondo** | Support | Community fundraising with direct, transparent giving |
| **Sulat** | Share | A moderated space for stories and expression |
| **Aral** | Learn | Study tools for Philippine licensure exam preparation |

Tanikala's source repositories stay private while the platform is under active
development.
